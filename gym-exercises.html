How to load it

// max force

Strength

Reps1–5
Load~85%+ 1RM
Sets3–5
Rest2–5 min
TempoLower controlled (2s) · drive up with intent / explosively
EffortHard, but leave 1–2 reps in the tank
// build muscle

Hypertrophy

Reps6–12
Load~67–80%
Sets3–4
Rest60–90 s
TempoSlow eccentric (3s down) · 1s squeeze · controlled up — chase tension
EffortNear failure (0–2 reps left) on the last set
// move fast

Power

Reps3–5
Load~50–70%
Sets3–5
Rest2–3 min
TempoQuick dip down · explode up as fast as you can · reset each rep
EffortStop the set the moment bar speed drops
// stamina + control

Endurance / Stability

Reps15+
Load<60% / body
Sets2–3
Rest30–60 s
TempoSteady & continuous (2s–2s) · no rest at top/bottom · build the burn
EffortControlled fatigue, never sloppy form
// tempo decoder A tempo like 3-1-1 reads eccentric – pause – concentric: e.g. 3s lowering, 1s pause, 1s lift. The rule of thumb across goals: control the lowering, then push back hard. A slow, deliberate eccentric builds muscle and protects joints; an explosive concentric (the lift) builds strength and power. X means "as fast as possible".
// runner's note In race build, keep heavy lower-body lifting off your key run + long-run days, and lean toward single-leg, foot and stability work. Save the big strength loads for the off-season.
01

Glutes · Hips · Flexors

🍑 Glutes, Hips & Flexors ›

Hip Thrust 🏃

Free
Set upUpper back across a bench, bar/pad over the hips, feet flat and shin vertical at the top.
MoveDrive through the heels, squeeze glutes hard, ribs down — pause 1s at the top, lower controlled.
WatchArching the lower back at lockout instead of finishing with the glutes.

Cable / Band Glute Kickback 🏃

CableBand
Set upAnkle strap or band on one foot, face the machine, slight hinge forward, hands for support.
MovePush the foot back and up, pause at full extension, return slowly without resting.
WatchSwinging from the lower back — keep the movement at the hip only.

Lateral Band Walk 🏃

BodyweightBand
Set upBand just above the knees, feet hip-width, drop into a quarter-squat, chest up.
MoveStep wide and slow side-to-side, keeping constant band tension — never let the feet snap together.
WatchKnees caving inward — keep them tracking over the toes.

Hip Abduction Machine 🏃

Machine
Set upSeated, pads against the outside of the knees, sit tall against the backrest.
MovePush the knees out, pause, then resist slowly on the way back in.
WatchLeaning the torso forward to throw the weight open.

Bulgarian Split Squat 🏃

FreeBodyweight
Set upStand a stride in front of a bench, lace the rear foot's laces on it, torso tall.
MoveDrop straight down until the front thigh is parallel, weight through the front heel, drive up.
WatchFront heel lifting or the knee shooting way past the toes.

Single-Leg Glute Bridge 🏃

Bodyweight
Set upOn your back, one foot planted close to the glutes, other leg extended or knee tucked.
MoveLift the hips until level, hold a beat at the top, lower slowly. Keep hips square.
WatchHips dropping or twisting toward the working side.

Standing Banded Hip Flexor March 🏃

BandBodyweight
Set upLoop a band low around one foot (anchored behind/below), stand tall, hand on a wall.
MoveDrive the knee up to hip height against the band, control it back down. This trains the psoas/hip flexor directly.
WatchLeaning back to lift the knee — stay upright and tall.

Seated Weighted Knee Raise 🏃

BodyweightFree
Set upSit tall on a bench edge, optionally rest a light plate/dumbbell on one thigh.
MoveLift the knee toward the chest as high as you can, pause, lower slow — strict hip-flexor isolation.
WatchRounding the back; keep the spine tall and the lift coming from the hip.
02

Quads

🦵 Quads ›

Back / Goblet Squat 🏃

FreeBodyweight
Set upFeet shoulder-width, toes slightly out; bar on upper back, or hold a dumbbell at the chest (goblet).
MoveBrace, sit between the hips, knees track over toes, lower to depth, drive up evenly through mid-foot.
WatchHeels lifting or the chest collapsing forward.

Leg Press 🏃

Machine
Set upSit back into the seat, feet mid-platform shoulder-width, lower back flat against the pad.
MoveLower under control until knees reach ~90°, press back without slamming the lockout.
WatchGoing so deep the lower back rounds off the pad.

Leg Extension

Machine
Set upKnees in line with the machine's pivot, shin pad on the lower shin, grip the handles.
MoveStraighten the legs, squeeze the quads at the top, lower slowly through full range.
WatchSwinging or kicking the weight up with momentum.

Walking Lunge 🏃

FreeBodyweight
Set upStand tall, optional dumbbells at the sides, eyes forward.
MoveTake a long step, drop the back knee toward the floor, push through the front heel to step on.
WatchShort choppy steps that load the knee instead of the whole leg.

Step-Up 🏃

BodyweightFree
Set upBox around knee height, place the whole working foot flat on top.
MoveDrive up through the top leg only, stand fully, lower under control — no push-off from the bottom foot.
WatchBouncing off the floor foot rather than working the top leg.
03

Hamstrings

🔙 Hamstrings ›

Romanian Deadlift (RDL) 🏃

Free
Set upStand holding a bar/dumbbells at the thighs, feet hip-width, soft knees, shoulders back.
MovePush the hips back, slide the weight down the legs, feel the hamstring stretch, then stand tall by driving hips forward.
WatchRounding the back, or bending the knees so it becomes a squat.

Seated / Lying Leg Curl 🏃

Machine
Set upPad on the lower calf/Achilles, knees at the pivot, strap or thigh pad locked down.
MoveCurl the pad in fully, squeeze the hamstring, resist slowly back out (a slow eccentric is gold here).
WatchHips lifting off the seat to gain leverage.

Nordic Curl (assisted) 🏃

Bodyweight
Set upKneel on a pad, ankles anchored under a bench/pad or held by a partner, body upright.
MoveLower the torso forward as slowly as you can control, hands ready to catch, then push back up.
WatchBending at the hips — keep a straight line from knee to shoulder.

Single-Leg RDL 🏃

FreeBodyweight
Set upStand on one leg, slight knee bend, weight in the opposite hand.
MoveHinge over the standing leg as the back leg extends behind, keep hips square, then return tall.
WatchThe hip opening out to the side — keep it level to the floor.

Cable / Band Pull-Through

CableBand
Set upFace away from a low cable/anchor, rope between the legs, hands holding it, hinge slightly.
MovePush hips back to load, then snap the hips through to stand tall and squeeze the glutes.
WatchLifting with the arms instead of driving from the hips.
04

Calves · Lower Leg

🦿 Calves & Lower Leg ›

Standing Calf Raise 🏃

MachineFreeBody
Set upBalls of the feet on a step/machine pad, heels hanging, legs straight, tall posture.
MoveRise as high as possible onto the toes, pause at the top, lower slowly into a deep stretch.
WatchBouncing through tiny partial reps with no top squeeze or bottom stretch.

Seated Calf Raise 🏃

MachineFree
Set upSeated, pad across the thighs (or DB on knees), balls of feet on the platform.
MoveBent knees target the deeper soleus — slow tempo, full range, big squeeze each rep.
WatchRushing it; this one rewards slow, deliberate reps.

Single-Leg Calf Raise 🏃

Bodyweight
Set upOne foot's ball on a step, other foot tucked, light fingers on a wall for balance.
MoveFull drop below the step then full rise onto the toes, controlling any side-to-side wobble.
WatchLetting the ankle roll out — keep pressure through the big toe.

Tibialis Raise (toes up) 🏃

BodyweightFree
Set upHeels on the floor (or back against a wall, feet out), legs fairly straight.
MovePull the toes up toward the shins as far as possible, squeeze the front of the shin, lower slow.
WatchSkipping it — strong tibialis muscles help fend off shin niggles.
05

Feet

🦶 Feet ›

Short-Foot / Arch Doming 🏃

Bodyweight
Set upSit or stand barefoot, foot flat, toes relaxed and long on the floor.
MoveWithout curling the toes, draw the ball of the foot toward the heel to raise the arch — hold 5s, release. Builds the foot's intrinsic muscles.
WatchScrunching the toes — the arch should lift on its own.

Toe Yoga (lift & split) 🏃

Bodyweight
Set upBarefoot, foot flat, all toes down on the floor.
MoveLift only the big toe while keeping the other four down — then reverse. Then practise spreading all toes apart. Improves foot control.
WatchFrustration — it's hard at first; even the attempt trains the wiring.

Towel Scrunch 🏃

Bodyweight
Set upSit with a small towel flat on the floor under a bare foot.
MoveUse the toes to scrunch and drag the towel toward you, then push it back out. Add a light weight on the towel to progress.
WatchLifting the heel — keep it planted, work only the toes.

Heel & Toe Walks 🏃

Bodyweight
Set upClear a short stretch of floor, stand tall.
MoveWalk ~15m on your heels (toes lifted) for the shins, then ~15m on tip-toes for the calves and feet.
WatchRushing — slow, deliberate steps load the muscles more.

Banded Ankle Inversion / Eversion 🏃

BandBodyweight
Set upSit with the leg out, a band looped around the forefoot and anchored to one side.
MoveTurn the sole inward against the band (inversion), then anchor the other side and turn it outward (eversion). Bulletproofs the ankle.
WatchMoving the whole leg — isolate the motion to the ankle.

Single-Leg Balance 🏃

Bodyweight
Set upStand barefoot on one foot, soft knee, gaze fixed ahead.
MoveHold 30–60s, feeling the foot grip and adjust. Progress: close the eyes, or stand on a folded mat/cushion.
WatchGripping with clawed toes — aim for a quiet, stable arch.
06

Core · Trunk

🎯 Core & Trunk ›

Plank 🏃

Bodyweight
Set upForearms under shoulders, legs extended, on toes — body in one straight line.
MoveSqueeze glutes, brace the abs as if braced for a punch, breathe steadily, hold for quality time.
WatchHips sagging or piking up — short solid holds beat long sloppy ones.

Side Plank 🏃

BodyweightFree
Set upOn one forearm, elbow under shoulder, feet stacked, hips and shoulders stacked vertically.
MoveLift the hips high, hold tall, keep the body in one line. Progress with a top-leg raise.
WatchHips drifting forward or sinking toward the floor.

Pallof Press 🏃

Cable
Set upStand side-on to a cable at chest height, hold the handle at the sternum, feet hip-width.
MovePress the handle straight out and resist the pull to rotate, hold 2s, return. Anti-rotation gold.
WatchLetting the torso twist toward the machine.

Dead Bug 🏃

Bodyweight
Set upOn your back, arms reaching to the ceiling, knees stacked over hips at 90°.
MoveSlowly lower the opposite arm and leg toward the floor, keeping the lower back flat, then switch.
WatchThe lower back arching off the floor as the limbs extend.

Hanging Knee Raise

BodyweightFree
Set upHang from a pull-up bar, shoulders slightly engaged (not fully slack).
MoveRaise the knees toward the chest with control, lower slowly, no swing. Progress to straight legs.
WatchUsing momentum to kip the legs up.
07

Back

🪝 Back ›

Pull-Up / Assisted Pull-Up

BodyweightMachine
Set upOverhand grip slightly wider than shoulders; knee on the pad if using the assist machine.
MoveFrom a dead hang, pull the elbows down and back to get the chin over the bar, lower with control.
WatchHalf reps — get the full stretch at the bottom each time.

Lat Pulldown

MachineCable
Set upThighs locked under the pad, wide grip, lean back just slightly, chest up.
MovePull the bar to the upper chest by driving the elbows down, control it back up to a full stretch.
WatchLeaning way back and rowing it instead of pulling down.

Bent-Over Row

Free
Set upHinge to ~45°, flat back, bar/dumbbells hanging at arm's length, core braced.
MoveRow to the lower ribs, squeeze the shoulder blades together, lower under control.
WatchRounding the back or standing up to heave the weight.

Seated Cable Row

CableMachine
Set upFeet on the plate, soft knees, tall chest, slight forward reach to start.
MovePull the handle to the belly, squeeze the blades together, return slowly to a full stretch.
WatchRocking back and forth from the hips for momentum.

Single-Arm Dumbbell Row

Free
Set upOne hand and knee on a bench, back flat and parallel to the floor, DB hanging.
MoveRow the dumbbell to the hip, keep the torso still, lower fully.
WatchTwisting the torso to swing up a weight that's too heavy.

Face Pull 🏃

CableBand
Set upRope at face height, hold with thumbs back, step back to tension, slight lean.
MovePull the rope to your forehead, hands splitting apart, squeeze the rear delts — great posture work.
WatchShrugging the traps up instead of pulling back and out.
08

Chest

💪 Chest ›

Barbell / Dumbbell Bench Press

Free
Set upLie back, shoulder blades pinched and down, feet planted, slight natural arch.
MoveLower to the mid-chest with control, then press up and slightly back over the shoulders.
WatchFlaring the elbows out to 90° — keep them tucked to ~45°.

Incline Dumbbell Press

Free
Set upBench at ~30°, dumbbells at the upper chest, wrists stacked over elbows.
MovePress up and slightly in, control the stretch at the bottom, don't clank the bells together.
WatchBench set too steep — it becomes a shoulder press.

Chest Press Machine

Machine
Set upAdjust the seat so the handles sit at mid-chest, back flat against the pad.
MovePress out smoothly to nearly straight (don't slam), return with control to a chest stretch.
WatchShoulders rolling forward off the pad at the start.

Cable / Pec Deck Fly

CableMachine
Set upHandles set to chest height, a soft fixed bend in the elbows, stand/sit tall.
MoveBring the hands together in a wide hugging arc, squeeze the chest, open slowly to a stretch.
WatchBending the elbows into a press instead of keeping a fly arc.

Push-Up

Bodyweight
Set upHands slightly wider than shoulders, body in a straight line, core and glutes tight.
MoveLower the chest to the floor with elbows ~45°, press back to lockout. Drop to knees to scale.
WatchHips sagging or only going halfway down.
09

Shoulders

🤸 Shoulders ›

Overhead / Shoulder Press

FreeMachine
Set upWeights at shoulder height, elbows under the wrists, core braced, ribs down.
MovePress straight overhead until the arms lock out with the biceps by the ears, lower with control.
WatchArching the lower back to push the weight up.

Lateral Raise

FreeCable
Set upStand tall, light weights at the sides, a slight fixed bend in the elbows.
MoveRaise out to the sides to shoulder height, leading with the elbows, lower slowly (3s).
WatchSwinging up with momentum or shrugging the traps.

Rear Delt Fly 🏃

FreeCable
Set upHinge forward at the hips, flat back, light weights hanging beneath the chest.
MoveRaise the arms out to the sides like wings, squeeze the rear delts, lower slowly.
WatchUsing the lower back to swing rather than the rear delts.

Front Raise

Free
Set upStand tall, weights resting on the front of the thighs, core braced.
MoveRaise to shoulder height in front, pause, lower with control.
WatchLeaning back and bouncing the weight up.

Arms

💥 Arms ›

Biceps Curl (DB / Bar / Cable)

FreeCable
Set upStand tall, elbows pinned to the sides, palms facing up, weights at arm's length.
MoveCurl up, squeeze at the top, lower slowly (3s) for tension — don't drop it.
WatchSwinging the elbows forward and using the back/shoulders.

Hammer Curl

Free
Set upDumbbells at the sides with a neutral grip (thumbs up, palms facing in).
MoveCurl up keeping the wrists straight, control the lowering — targets the forearm and outer arm.
WatchRushing the eccentric (the lowering phase).

Triceps Pushdown

Cable
Set upFace a high cable with a rope/bar, elbows tucked to the ribs, slight forward lean.
MovePush down to full extension, squeeze the triceps, return only to where the elbows stay tucked.
WatchElbows flaring out and drifting forward.

Overhead Triceps Extension

Free
Set upHold one dumbbell with both hands overhead, elbows pointing up and forward.
MoveLower the weight behind the head for a deep stretch, then extend back overhead.
WatchElbows flaring wide and losing the stretch.

Dips / Assisted Dips

BodyweightMachine
Set upGrip parallel bars (or use the assist pad), arms locked out, body slightly upright for triceps.
MoveLower under control with elbows tracking back, press to a strong lockout.
WatchShrugging up into the shoulders at the bottom — keep the chest proud.
