RIR (reps in reserve): for most growth work, stop 1–2 reps short of failure on the last set — clean reps beat grinding.

Runner note: in race build, keep heavy lower-body lifting away from key run/long-run days, and lean toward single-leg + stability work. Off-season is the time to push strength loads.

🍑 Glutes & Hips ›

Hip Thrust 🏃

Free

Bar/pad on hips, drive through heels, squeeze glutes hard at the top, ribs down.

Watch out: overarching the lower back at lockout instead of finishing with the glutes.

Cable / Band Glute Kickback 🏃

CableBodyweight

Hinge slightly, push the foot back and up, pause at full extension.

Watch out: swinging from the lower back — keep the move at the hip.

Lateral Band Walk 🏃

BodyweightBand

Band above knees, half-squat, step wide and slow, keep tension the whole time.

Watch out: knees caving inward — keep them tracking over the toes.

Hip Abduction Machine 🏃

Machine

Push knees out against the pads, slow on the way back in.

Watch out: leaning the torso forward to cheat the weight.

Bulgarian Split Squat 🏃

FreeBodyweight

Rear foot on bench, drop straight down, weight through the front heel.

Watch out: front knee drifting past the toes / heel lifting.

Single-Leg Glute Bridge 🏃

Bodyweight

One foot planted, lift hips level, hold a beat at the top.

Watch out: hips dropping or twisting — keep them square.

🦵 Quads ›

Back / Goblet Squat 🏃

FreeBodyweight

Brace, sit between the hips, knees track over toes, drive up evenly.

Watch out: heels lifting or chest collapsing forward.

Leg Press 🏃

Machine

Feet mid-platform, lower until ~90°, press without locking out hard.

Watch out: letting the lower back round off the pad at the bottom.

Leg Extension

Machine

Squeeze quads to straighten, control the lowering, full range.

Watch out: swinging/kicking the weight up with momentum.

Walking Lunge 🏃

FreeBodyweight

Long step, drop the back knee, push through the front heel to stand.

Watch out: short choppy steps that load the knee instead of the leg.

Step-Up 🏃

BodyweightFree

Whole foot on a knee-height box, drive up through that leg, no push-off.

Watch out: pushing off the bottom foot instead of working the top leg.

🔙 Hamstrings ›

Romanian Deadlift (RDL) 🏃

Free

Soft knees, push hips back, bar close to legs, feel the stretch, stand tall.

Watch out: rounding the back or squatting it instead of hinging.

Seated / Lying Leg Curl 🏃

Machine

Curl the pad in fully, squeeze, resist slowly on the way back.

Watch out: hips lifting off the seat to gain leverage.

Nordic Curl (assisted) 🏃

Bodyweight

Anchor ankles, lower as slowly as you can control, hands ready to catch.

Watch out: bending at the hips — keep a straight line knee to shoulder.

Single-Leg RDL 🏃

FreeBodyweight

Hinge over one leg, back leg extends behind, hips stay square.

Watch out: hip opening out to the side — keep it level to the floor.

Cable / Band Pull-Through

CableBand

Face away, rope between legs, hinge back then snap hips through.

Watch out: using the arms to lift instead of the hips.

🦶 Calves & Lower Leg ›

Standing Calf Raise 🏃

MachineFreeBodyweight

Rise high onto the balls of the feet, pause, lower under control for a stretch.

Watch out: bouncing through partial reps with no top squeeze.

Seated Calf Raise 🏃

MachineFree

Knees bent loads the soleus — slow tempo, full range each rep.

Watch out: rushing; this one rewards slow, deliberate reps.

Single-Leg Calf Raise 🏃

Bodyweight

One leg on a step, full drop and full rise, light fingers on a wall for balance.

Watch out: wobbling at the ankle — control side-to-side too.

Tibialis Raise (toes up) 🏃

BodyweightFree

Heels on floor/edge, pull toes up toward shins, squeeze the front of the shin.

Watch out: skipping it — strong tibs help with shin niggles for runners.

🎯 Core & Trunk ›

Plank 🏃

Bodyweight

Straight line head to heels, squeeze glutes and brace, breathe.

Watch out: hips sagging or piking up — quality time beats long sloppy holds.

Side Plank 🏃

BodyweightFree

Stack hips and shoulders, lift hips high, hold tall.

Watch out: hips drifting forward or sinking toward the floor.

Pallof Press 🏃

Cable

Cable to the side, press straight out and resist the pull, stay square.

Watch out: letting the torso rotate toward the machine.

Dead Bug 🏃

Bodyweight

Lower back flat, extend opposite arm and leg slowly, then switch.

Watch out: lower back arching off the floor as the limbs extend.

Cable / Machine Crunch

CableMachine

Curl the ribs toward the hips, controlled, no neck yanking.

Watch out: pulling with the hip flexors instead of the abs.

Hanging Knee Raise

BodyweightFree

Hang from the bar, raise knees with control, no swing.

Watch out: using momentum to kip the legs up.

🪝 Back ›

Pull-Up / Assisted Pull-Up

BodyweightMachine

Start from a dead hang, pull elbows down and back, chin over the bar.

Watch out: half reps — get the full stretch at the bottom.

Lat Pulldown

MachineCable

Pull the bar to the upper chest, drive elbows down, chest proud.

Watch out: leaning way back and rowing it instead of pulling down.

Bent-Over Row

Free

Hinge ~45°, flat back, row to the lower ribs, squeeze the shoulder blades.

Watch out: rounding the back or standing up to heave the weight.

Seated Cable Row

CableMachine

Tall chest, pull to the belly, shoulder blades together, slow return.

Watch out: rocking back and forth from the hips.

Single-Arm Dumbbell Row

Free

Hand and knee on bench, row the dumbbell to the hip, no twist.

Watch out: rotating the torso to lift heavier than you can control.

Face Pull

CableBand

Rope to face height, pull to the forehead, thumbs back, squeeze rear delts.

Watch out: shrugging the traps up instead of pulling back.

💪 Chest ›

Barbell / Dumbbell Bench Press

Free

Shoulder blades pinched, lower to mid-chest, press up and slightly back.

Watch out: flaring elbows out to 90° — keep them ~45°.

Incline Dumbbell Press

Free

Bench ~30°, press up and in, control the stretch at the bottom.

Watch out: bench too steep — it turns into a shoulder press.

Chest Press Machine

Machine

Handles at mid-chest, press smoothly, don't slam the lockout.

Watch out: shoulders rolling forward off the pad.

Cable / Pec Deck Fly

CableMachine

Soft elbows, bring hands together in a hugging arc, squeeze the chest.

Watch out: bending the elbows into a press instead of a fly.

Push-Up

Bodyweight

Body in a straight line, lower chest to floor, elbows ~45°.

Watch out: hips sagging or only going halfway down.

🤷 Shoulders ›

Overhead / Shoulder Press

FreeMachine

Brace core, press straight overhead, ribs down, finish with biceps by ears.

Watch out: arching the lower back to push the weight up.

Lateral Raise

FreeCable

Slight bend in elbows, raise to shoulder height, lead with the elbows.

Watch out: swinging up with momentum or shrugging the traps.

Rear Delt Fly

FreeCable

Hinge forward, raise arms out to the sides, squeeze the rear delts.

Watch out: using the lower back to swing rather than the rear delts.

Front Raise

Free

Raise the weight to shoulder height in front, controlled down.

Watch out: leaning back and bouncing the weight up.

💥 Arms ›

Biceps Curl (DB / Bar / Cable)

FreeCable

Elbows pinned to sides, curl up, squeeze, lower slowly.

Watch out: swinging the elbows forward and using the back.

Hammer Curl

Free

Neutral grip (thumbs up), curl up keeping wrists straight.

Watch out: rushing the lowering phase.

Triceps Pushdown

Cable

Elbows tucked, push down to full extension, squeeze the triceps.

Watch out: elbows flaring out and drifting forward.

Overhead Triceps Extension

Free

Weight behind the head, extend overhead, keep elbows pointing up.

Watch out: elbows flaring wide and losing the stretch.

Dips / Assisted Dips

BodyweightMachine

Lower under control, elbows back for triceps, press to lockout.

Watch out: shrugging up into the shoulders at the bottom.
