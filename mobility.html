Gym Field Manual · No.02

Breathe
& Open.

Mobility, stretch & release — yoga-rooted. For warm-ups, cooldowns, or the days the body just needs space. 🏃 marks the runner-keepers.

Train Mobility
// how to use Before a session: move dynamically — flow in and out of positions, 5–8 slow reps. After / on rest days: hold longer (5–10 slow breaths) and sink into the stretch. Foam rolling: 30–60s per spot, breathe out as you settle on a tender point — pressure should feel like a "good ache", never sharp.
01

Hips · Flexors

🌀 Hips & Flexors ›

Couch Stretch (hip flexor) 🏃

No kitWall
Set upKneel facing away from a wall/couch, shin of the back leg up against it, front foot planted ahead in a lunge.
OpenTuck the pelvis under and squeeze the back glute to drive the stretch into the front of the hip/thigh.
Hold5–8 breaths each side. Brilliant antidote to a desk and to mile after mile.
WatchLetting the lower back arch — the pelvic tuck is what makes it work.

Pigeon Pose 🏃

No kit
Set upFrom all fours, bring one shin forward across the mat, back leg long behind, hips square.
OpenStay upright for a lighter version, or walk the hands forward and fold over the front shin to deepen.
Hold8–10 slow breaths each side — targets deep glute/piriformis.
WatchCollapsing onto one hip; prop the front-leg hip on a block/cushion if it floats.

90/90 Hip Switch 🏃

No kit
Set upSit with the front leg bent at 90° in front, back leg bent at 90° out to the side.
OpenSit tall, lean gently over the front shin; to mobilise, rotate the knees over to switch to the other side and repeat.
Hold5 breaths each side, or flow 8–10 slow switches.
WatchRounding the back — keep the chest lifted.

Deep Squat / Malasana 🏃

No kit
Set upFeet a little wider than hips, toes slightly out, sink the hips down toward the heels.
OpenElbows inside the knees, press gently out, lift the chest. Rock side to side to find space in the hips and ankles.
Hold5–10 breaths. Hold a doorframe/rack if you tip backward.
WatchHeels lifting — sit them down, or rest them on a small wedge.

Figure-4 (supine) 🏃

No kit
Set upOn your back, cross one ankle over the opposite thigh to make a "4".
OpenThread the hands behind the bottom thigh and gently draw it toward the chest.
Hold6–8 breaths each side — gentle glute/piriformis release.
WatchPushing into pain; the crossed knee should ease open, not be forced.
02

Hamstrings · Posterior

🌿 Hamstrings & Posterior ›

Standing / Seated Forward Fold 🏃

No kit
Set upStanding feet hip-width (or seated legs long), hinge from the hips, soft knees.
OpenLet the head and arms hang heavy, lengthen the back of the legs with each exhale. Micro-bend the knees to protect the lower back.
Hold8–10 breaths, gently nodding "yes/no" to release the neck.
WatchYanking down with a rounded, locked spine — fold from the hips.

Downward Dog 🏃

No kit
Set upFrom all fours, tuck the toes and lift the hips up and back into an inverted V.
OpenPress the chest toward the thighs, lengthen the spine; bend one knee then the other to "pedal" the calves and hamstrings.
Hold5–8 breaths, or pedal it out dynamically.
WatchRounding the upper back to force the heels down — a long spine matters more than flat heels.

Supine Hamstring Stretch (strap/wall) 🏃

No kitStrap
Set upOn your back, loop a strap/towel around one foot (or use a doorway), other leg long on the floor.
OpenRaise the strapped leg toward vertical with a soft knee, draw gently until you feel the stretch.
Hold6–8 breaths each side. Lower-back-friendly way to target hamstrings.
WatchThe bottom leg/lower back lifting off the floor.
03

Spine · T-Spine

🦎 Spine & T-Spine ›

Cat–Cow 🏃

No kit
Set upOn all fours, wrists under shoulders, knees under hips, spine neutral.
OpenInhale: drop the belly, lift the chest and tail (cow). Exhale: round the spine, tuck the chin and tail (cat). Flow with the breath.
Hold8–10 slow cycles — the best wake-up for the whole spine.
WatchRushing — let each breath drive the movement.

Thread the Needle

No kit
Set upFrom all fours, reach one arm up to the ceiling, opening the chest.
OpenThread that arm under the body, lowering the shoulder and temple toward the mat, then rewind and open back up.
Hold5 breaths threaded each side — gentle upper-back rotation.
WatchCrushing the neck — let the shoulder, not the head, take the weight.

Open Book (T-spine rotation)

No kit
Set upLie on one side, knees stacked and bent 90°, arms stretched out together in front.
OpenKeeping the knees down, sweep the top arm up and over to the other side, following the hand with the eyes; return.
Hold6–8 slow sweeps each side, exhaling into the open position.
WatchKnees popping up — keep them anchored so rotation comes from the upper back.

Cobra / Sphinx

No kit
Set upLie face down; sphinx = forearms down, elbows under shoulders. Cobra = hands by the ribs.
OpenGently lift the chest, lengthen the front of the body, keep the shoulders down away from the ears.
Hold5–8 breaths — a counter to all-day flexion and hunching.
WatchCranking the neck back; keep it long, gaze slightly down.
04

Shoulders · Chest

🕊️ Shoulders & Chest ›

Doorway Pec Stretch

DoorwayNo kit
Set upForearm on a doorframe, elbow around shoulder height, step the same-side foot through.
OpenGently turn the chest away to open the front of the shoulder and pec. Adjust elbow height to hit different fibres.
Hold5–8 breaths each side.
WatchOver-rotating into pain — it should feel like a broad opening, not a pinch.

Shoulder CARs (controlled circles)

No kit
Set upStand tall, one arm by the side, ribs down, core lightly braced.
OpenTrace the largest slow circle you can with the arm — forward, up, back, down — exploring the full range with control.
Hold3–5 slow circles each direction, each side.
WatchThe body twisting to cheat range — keep the torso still, move only the shoulder.

Child's Pose with Reach

No kit
Set upKneel and sit the hips back toward the heels, arms stretched long in front on the floor.
OpenWalk the hands forward and to each side, pressing the chest toward the floor to open the lats and shoulders.
Hold8–10 breaths — also a lovely lower-back decompression.
WatchShrugging the shoulders to the ears — keep them drawing back.
05

Ankles · Calves

🦶 Ankles & Calves ›

Knee-to-Wall Ankle Rock 🏃

WallNo kit
Set upKneel/half-kneel facing a wall, front foot a few inches back from it, toes pointing forward.
OpenDrive the knee forward over the toes to touch the wall, keeping the heel down. Edge the foot back to make it harder.
Hold8–10 slow rocks each side — key ankle range for running and squatting.
WatchThe heel popping up, or the knee caving in — keep it tracking over the toes.

Wall / Step Calf Stretch 🏃

Wall / Step
Set upHands on a wall, one foot back and straight (gastroc) — then repeat with a bent back knee (soleus).
OpenPress the back heel down and lean in. The bent-knee version reaches the deeper, lower calf.
Hold5–8 breaths each variation, each side.
WatchThe back foot turning out — keep the toes pointing straight ahead.

Ankle CARs (controlled circles) 🏃

No kit
Set upSeated or standing, lift one foot slightly off the floor.
OpenTrace slow, full circles with the foot, pointing and flexing through every edge of the range.
Hold5 circles each direction — great pre-run ankle prep.
WatchMoving the whole shin — isolate it to the ankle joint.
06

Roll · Release

🧴 Foam Rolling & Pressure Points ›

Quad Roll 🏃

Roller
Set upFace down, roller under the front of one thigh, forearms supporting you in a plank.
RollRoll slowly from above the knee to the hip; pause and breathe on tender spots, slightly bend/straighten the knee to floss.
Time30–60s each leg.
WatchRolling fast and bouncy — slow pressure does the work.

TFL / Side-Hip Roll 🏃

Roller
Set upOn your side, roller just below the hip bone on the meaty front-side of the hip (the TFL).
RollWork this small area slowly — it often refers tightness down the IT band, so target the source here rather than grinding the band itself.
Time30–45s each side.
WatchHammering the bony outer thigh/IT band directly — ease off if it's sharp.

Glute / Piriformis Ball 🏃

Ball
Set upSit on a massage/lacrosse ball, that ankle crossed over the opposite knee to open the glute.
RollHunt for the tender knot, then rest on it and breathe slowly until it softens (30s+).
Time45–60s each side — gold for runners' deep-hip tightness.
WatchNumbness/tingling down the leg — you're on the nerve; shift off it.

Calf Roll 🏃

Roller
Set upSeated, one calf on the roller, the other foot crossed on top for extra pressure, hands lifting the hips.
RollRoll ankle-to-knee slowly, and rotate the leg in and out to reach the inner and outer calf.
Time30–60s each leg.
WatchHolding your breath — exhale into the tender spots.

T-Spine Extension over Roller

Roller
Set upRoller across the upper back, knees bent, hands supporting the head, hips low.
RollGently arch back over the roller, then inch it up a little and repeat — opens a hunched upper back.
Time3–5 gentle extensions at a couple of levels.
WatchCranking the lower back — keep the movement in the upper/mid back only.

Foot / Arch Ball Release 🏃

Ball
Set upStand or sit, place a small firm ball under the arch of a bare foot.
RollRoll heel-to-toe with gentle pressure, pausing on tight spots. Eases the plantar fascia and the whole posterior chain.
Time30–60s each foot — especially good after long runs.
WatchToo much pressure on an already-sore plantar fascia; keep it gentle.
07

Quick Flows

🌊 Quick Flows ›

World's Greatest Stretch 🏃

No kit
Set upStep into a deep forward lunge, both hands framing the front foot.
FlowDrop the back knee & sink the hips → rotate the inside arm up to the ceiling (open the chest) → place it down and straighten the front leg to fold over it. Step back, switch sides.
Reps3–5 per side — the single best full-body warm-up before a run or lift.
WatchRushing the rotation; pause and breathe at the open position.

Sun Salutation (gentle) 🏃

No kit
Set upStand tall at the front of the mat, feet together, breath steady.
FlowInhale arms up → exhale fold → inhale half-lift → step/float back to plank → lower → cobra → downward dog (5 breaths) → step forward → rise. Link each move to a breath.
Reps3–5 rounds to build heat and mobilise head-to-toe.
WatchHolding the breath — the breath leads, the body follows.

Hip Opener Flow 🏃

No kit
Set upStart in a deep squat (malasana).
FlowSquat → hands down, straighten legs into a forward fold → step one foot back to a low lunge → rotate open → return → switch. Cycle slowly.
Reps3–4 slow rounds — frees the hips after sitting or running.
WatchForcing depth — let the range grow over the rounds.
General mobility guidance — not medical advice.
Stretch to a "good" tension, never sharp pain.
